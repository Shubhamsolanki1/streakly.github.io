# Daily Streak

A free daily planner that runs entirely in your browser. Set daily targets, tick them off, and keep your streak alive.

**Live app:** _add your GitHub Pages link here once it's deployed, e.g. `https://Shubhamsolanki1.github.io/daily-streak/`_

## Features
- **Daily targets** in your own sections, with priorities and the weekdays each task repeats
- **Carry-over:** unfinished targets move to tomorrow (can be turned off)
- **Streaks, weekly/monthly goals** and a daily progress card
- **Pomodoro timer** with sound/notification and per-task focus time
- **Insights:** completion graphs, per-task charts, habit heatmaps, Pomodoro stats
- **Workspace:** rich notes and an endless **Plan Board** with stickies, arrows, drawing, undo/redo
- **Focus mode** that hides all edit buttons so you can just get things done
- **Settings:** 5 themes, text size, compact view, day start time, and more
- **Backup:** download your data as `.json` and restore it anywhere; export history as `.csv` and notes as `.md`
- A built-in **app tour** for new users

## Your data stays with you
Everything is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. There is no account and no server, and nobody else can see your data. Because of that, **download a backup now and then** (Settings, then Your data). Clearing your browser data erases the app's data.

## Use it
Open the live link, or download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox). It needs an internet connection only to load the fonts.

## Host your own copy (GitHub Pages)
1. Create a public repository and upload `index.html`.
2. Go to **Settings, Pages**, choose branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`, then save.
3. Your app will be live at `https://Shubhamsolanki1.github.io/<repo-name>/` in a minute or two — replace `<repo-name>` with whatever you name the repository, and update the **Live app** link above once it's live.
