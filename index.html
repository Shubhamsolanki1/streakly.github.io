Turn on Focus mode?

A calm mode for just getting things done. While it is on:

  • You can't add sections, goals or tasks.
  • You can't edit or delete anything. All edit, delete and add icons disappear.
  • You can still track progress: counters, Finish, Mark done, Pomodoro and Insights keep working.

Switch it off any time from the top bar and everything comes back exactly as it was.
